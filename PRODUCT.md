# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary audience:** Kenyan citizens — youth, civic activists, students, journalists, and public servants who want to understand and engage with Kenya's national budget.

- **Youth & students:** First-time budget readers. Need entry points that are visual, snackable, and socially shareable.
- **Civic activists & journalists:** Need depth, data verifiability, and context to hold government accountable.
- **Public servants & policy staff:** Reference users who need accurate, up-to-date budget breakdowns they can cite.
- **Context:** Arrive via social media, search, or direct links. Sessions vary from 30-second scrolls to deep research dives.

## Product Purpose

Budget Ndio Story translates Kenya's complex national budget into clear, actionable civic narratives. It makes fiscal literacy accessible and democratic participation tangible. Success looks like citizens who can name their county's allocation, understand what BETA priorities mean, and show up to public participation forums informed.

## Brand Personality

Bold, credible, warm.

- **Bold** — confident use of typography and color. No timid design. The budget is a big topic; the design matches its scale.
- **Credible** — data-first, but not dry. Every claim is sourced from National Treasury and Parliament. The design communicates trust without being boring.
- **Warm** — Kenyan, youth-led, human. The brand speaks to people, not at them. Warmth in tone, micro-copy, and human imagery.

## Anti-references

- **Not a government portal.** No official-looking blues, shields, crests, or bureaucratic density.
- **Not a Western NGO site.** No stock photos of african farmers, no "charity case" aesthetics.
- **Not a generic "modern SaaS."** No teal + purple gradients, no glassmorphism, no hero-metric templates.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers tell stories.** Data is the hero. Every visualization, every typographic choice serves the numbers. Let the figures breathe.
2. **Start with why.** Before any chart or table, the user must understand why it matters. Thin hierarchy is the enemy of civic literacy.
3. **Bold but grounded.** The palette is energetic but never garish. Typography has gravity. Motion is purposeful.
4. **Mobile-first, content-forward.** Most users arrive on phone. Content is the interface — no heavy chrome, no decorative bloat.
5. **Kenyan without cliché.** The brand is unmistakably Kenyan through data, color, and voice — not through flags, patterns, or tropes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text).
- Kiswahili and Sheng content support.
- Reduced motion respected.
- Color is never the sole carrier of meaning.
- Data visualizations include text alternatives and labels.
